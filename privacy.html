Privacy Policy

Effective 29 October 2026 · Operated by Murat Göğebakan

Information used by the app

Kapsül creates an anonymous Firebase account. The service stores its identifier, followed companies, read/unread and saved-item choices, and related timestamps. If you enable notifications, it also stores a push-notification token, device platform and delivery status. These records provide your inbox, watchlist, saved items and notifications.

Local information

Search and filter choices, cached announcements and changes awaiting synchronization are kept on your device. Reading and saved-item changes are sent to the service when connected. If you email support, we receive the information you choose to include and use it to handle your request.

Service providers and AI

Google Firebase provides authentication, storage and backend hosting. Expo helps deliver push notifications. These providers process technical information needed to run their services. OpenAI is used to summarize public KAP disclosure text; your watchlist, reading state and saved-item records are not included in those model requests. Providers may process data outside your country.

Advertising and tracking

Kapsül does not sell your data or use advertising identifiers for cross-app tracking. This version has no advertising or analytics/crash-reporting SDK. The app does not request your location, contacts, photos, payment details or investment holdings. Service providers may maintain operational and security logs.

Retention and your choices

Account and activity records are retained to provide the service unless removed following a verified request. Delivery records and operational logs may remain for service reliability or security. You can change your followed companies, remove saved items and disable notifications. Uninstalling removes local app data but does not automatically delete server records.

For access, correction or deletion requests, contact gogebakan.cagan@gmail.com. We may ask for information needed to identify the relevant anonymous account.
